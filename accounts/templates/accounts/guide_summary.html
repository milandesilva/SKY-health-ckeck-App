Summary Panel Guide
What is the Summary Panel?
The Summary Panel allows you to view statistics about health check votes across your department, team, or the whole organization. You can see vote distributions, trends, and analyze performance in a simple, visual way.

Anyone can use this page! It's designed to help you make better decisions based on real team feedback.
How to Use the Summary Panel
  1. Choose Filters: Use the dropdown menus at the top to select Department, Team, Session, Card, or a specific time period.
  2. View the Results: The panel updates automatically to show statistics for your selected criteria.
  3. Understand the Charts: Bar charts and tables help you quickly see how many people voted Green, Amber, or Red, and how trends are changing over time.
Tip: You can see more or less detail by adjusting the dropdowns – try "All Departments" or drill down to just your own team.
What Do the Filters Do?
FilterWhat it changes

CardFilter results for a single health card, or all cards.

Understanding the Voting Statistics
The main chart and table show how many people voted:
  • Green: Everything is good.
  • Amber: There are some issues or risks.
  • Red: There are serious problems or concerns.
Total Votes at the bottom tells you how many responses were included in your stats.
Understanding Trends
Each vote also includes a trend showing if the situation is:
  • Improving: Things are getting better compared to last time.
  • About the Same: No big changes since last time.
  • Getting Worse: Things have declined since last time.
The Trend Statistics chart and table show how many users picked each trend.
Overall Trend is based on the most selected trend type.
Example Scenario
Example: If you select your team and last month's date range, you might see:
  • 10 Green, 4 Amber, 1 Red votes
  • Trends: 8 Improving, 5 About the Same, 2 Getting Worse
Interpretation:
Most team members think things are going well, and the majority feel that things are getting better!
Tips & Best Practices
  • Check different filters to see if some teams or cards have more concerns than others.
  • Use the date filter to see if things are getting better or worse over time.
  • Look for patterns – if “Red” or “Getting Worse” increases, it might be time to take action.
  • If you are unsure, ask for help understanding the results!
