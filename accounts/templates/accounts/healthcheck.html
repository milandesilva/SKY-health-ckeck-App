Team Health Check
Welcome to your Team Health Check!

The Team Health Check is a quick survey designed to help your team reflect on how things are going, and to surface early signs of problems or successes.
  1. Click the Start Health Check button below when your team’s session is open.
  2. For each Health Check card, select a colored “traffic light” (Red, Amber, Green) and note if things are getting better, worse, or are steady.
  3. Optionally, add a comment to provide extra context.
  4. Use Next and Previous to move between cards at any time.
  5. You must select both a color and a trend before moving to the next card or submitting your answers.
  6. You can Save and Exit at any time—your progress will be saved and you can resume later.
  7. Once you finish and submit, you will receive a confirmation and your feedback will be securely stored.
Important:
  • You cannot edit your responses after submission.
  • If the button is greyed out, you have either finished this session or there is no active session for your team.
  • All responses are confidential and only shown in aggregate team summaries.
Thank you for your honest and thoughtful feedback!
